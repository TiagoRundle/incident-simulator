**Warning:** the datasets in this repository contain disturbing details about violent shootings in schools. Viewer discretion is advised.

BACKGROUND

This project was completed to support research on autonomous robotic systems that are designed to impede active shooters in schools.

The purpose is to document the events of school shootings at high-fidelity, so that data can be used to train models that better predict shooter movements.

This work was funded by a grant from the National Science Foundation.

DATASETS

This repository contains spatiotemporal data that tracks the events of historical school shootings second-by-second. The data records estimated (X,Y) grid positions across time for both shooters and victims. It also records the estimated times of shots, injuries, and deaths.

The repository contains datasets for 9 historical school shootings. These are:

2002 Erfurt School
2005 Red Lake
2007 Virginia Tech
2012 Sandy Hook
2017 Aztec High
2018 Kerch Polytechnic
2021 Oxford High
2024 Apalachee High
2025 Florida State

The timelines were reconstructed from available investigative reports and official documents. Most gaps in the timeline were filled by linear interpolation. Others were filled with estimates that were deemed most plausible given the available data. For these reasons, the resulting timelines are not perfect and are prone to mistakes of varying degrees.
